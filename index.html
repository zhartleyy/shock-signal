Trump's FY2027 budget proposes $1.5 trillion in defense spending — a 40% increase and the highest in modern history. Key allocations: $2.5B for missiles & munitions, $65.8B for 34 new ships, and initial funding for the "Golden Dome" missile defense system. Defense billionaire fortunes up $28B in Q1.

🤖 AI Chip Arms Race

NVIDIA's Rubin platform enters full production for H2 2026 cloud deployments. Huawei's 950PR inference chip is drawing massive orders from ByteDance and Alibaba, pushing China to 41% of the AI accelerator market. TSMC's 3nm capacity is sold out through 2028, triggering a GigaFab expansion in Arizona.

🏭 Post-Liberation Day Trade Regime

One year after "Liberation Day," tariffs are being restructured but remain elevated. USMCA auto exemptions extended indefinitely. China-US Paris talks produced no breakthrough. Global trade is reorganizing without the US, as other nations tear down barriers among themselves. Average US household cost: $1,500/year.

USMCA Auto Exemption 50+ Trade Actions $1,500/Household

🏦 Monetary Policy Uncertainty

The Fed holds rates at 3.5–3.75% with inflation still elevated from the oil shock. Only one cut expected this year. Fed Chair Powell's term expires in May 2026, injecting succession uncertainty. Consumer sentiment has plunged to its third-lowest reading ever — below the threshold of every recession since 1980.
