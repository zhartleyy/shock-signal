How Catalyst Works

Methodology
Step 01

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news across geopolitics, trade, energy, technology, finance, and regulatory channels to surface events from the past 24 hours.

1
Step 02

Trace

For each event, Catalyst identifies the supply or demand mechanism — what gets scarcer or more abundant — and traces the causal chain to specific publicly traded equities.

2
Step 03

Score

Signals are scored 1–10 on conviction based on: directness of the causal link, revenue exposure of named companies, and how much of the signal is already reflected in prices.

3
Step 04

Deliver

Results are delivered daily as a structured intelligence brief — winners and losers for each signal, grouped by macro theme, with double-beneficiary highlights surfaced automatically.
