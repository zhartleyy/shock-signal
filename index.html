Hormuz Tanker Traffic Craters, VLCC Freight Rates Spike

↑ Freight Rate Spike

Vessel crossings of the Strait of Hormuz collapsed from a pre-war norm of 120-140 daily transits to as few as single digits, with Lloyd's List counting just 53 transits in the week through July 20, down 66% from 157 a week earlier. VLCC freight rates from the Gulf to China jumped 24% to $1.67/bbl in a single session as insurers price in war risk, with marine war-risk premiums surging toward 1.25% of hull value. The dislocation lifts spot tanker owners while raising landed costs for import-dependent retailers.

China Expands Rare Earth Export Controls to 10 US and 14 EU Firms

↓ Supply Deficit

Beijing's MOFCOM tightened export restrictions on 10 U.S. companies on June 22 and blocked dual-use shipments to 14 EU firms by July 24, with a new July 1 reporting-and-reward rule formalizing enforcement against violators. DRAM Contract Prices Surge Up to 90% as AI Data Centers Consume HBM Capacity

↑ Demand Surge

SK Hynix and Samsung report HBM, DRAM and NAND capacity essentially sold out for 2026, with DRAM contract prices up roughly 64% quarter-on-quarter in Q2 and NAND up about 60%; Samsung's 32GB DDR5 module pricing has jumped 60% since September. Micron notes HBM production consumes roughly three times the wafer capacity of standard DRAM per gigabyte, forcing a structural reallocation away from consumer and PC-grade memory. Global DRAM supply growth in 2026 is pegged at just 16% versus historical norms of 20-30%, extending the shortage into 2027 as new fabs won't reach volume until then.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
