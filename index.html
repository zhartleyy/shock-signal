China's Rare Earth Export Truce Set to Expire November 10

Rate Constraint — Looming Supply Risk

The one-year suspension of China's sweeping October 2025 rare earth export controls — covering 12 of 17 elements and a 0.1% de minimis extraterritorial rule touching magnets and sputtering targets — is scheduled to expire November 10, just a month away. China controls roughly 88% of light and 97% of heavy rare earth oxide separation capacity, versus only about 15,000 tonnes of US annual separation capacity against 45,000 tonnes of domestic demand.

Hurricane Isaias Shuts In 63% of Gulf Oil Production

↓ Supply Disruption

Category 3 Hurricane Isaias forced the shut-in of roughly 1.28 million bpd (63%) of Gulf of Mexico oil output and 1.13 Bcf/d (57%) of natural gas production, with 121 of 371 manned platforms evacuated. The storm threatens Chevron's Pascagoula and Vertex's Saraland refineries, representing 2.4% of US refining capacity, arriving as the Strategic Petroleum Reserve sits at its lowest level since 1982 and fuel inventories have already been tapped by the overseas war-driven shortage.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
