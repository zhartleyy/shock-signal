USTR Imposes 10-12.5% Forced-Labor Tariffs on 60 Trading Partners

↑ Input Cost Inflation

New Section 301 tariffs of 10% to 12.5% took effect July 24 on 60 trading partners including Canada, Mexico, India, the UK and the EU, replacing the expired 10% global tariff and covering roughly 99% of all US imports. Officials framed the action as targeting forced-labor violations, but importers will bear the added duty cost, risking a fresh round of consumer price increases just as inflation is already running hot. Retailers and apparel brands sourcing from lower-cost manufacturing hubs face the most direct margin pressure.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
