Iran Mines Three Tankers in 48-Hour Hormuz Barrage

↑ Supply Risk Premium

Iran's navy and IRGC said three more oil tankers caught fire after striking naval mines in the eastern Strait of Hormuz over Nov 13-14, continuing a pace of attacks that saw 10 ships hit in the first 10 days of November after a record 37 attacks in October. The US military says it has cleared the strait's main routes of mines, but Tehran disputes this and continues restricting transit since the war began in February. Roughly one-fifth of global oil consumption normally moves through the waterway.

Cold Snap Sparks Natural Gas Short Squeeze, LNG Feedgas Hits Record

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
