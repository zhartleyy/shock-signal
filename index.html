AI Power CrunchEnergy / Technology

IEA: Data Center Power Demand to Double by 2030, $4T AI Buildout

↑ Demand Surge

The IEA's World Energy Outlook 2026 projects global data center electricity demand doubling to roughly 1,000 terawatt-hours a year by 2030, driven by $4 trillion in AI infrastructure spending and 93 large 'AI factory' sites totaling about 50 GW. The agency says gas and coal will supply roughly 60% of actual generation despite renewable power-purchase agreements covering nearly half of contracted capacity, because reliability requirements favor firm generation. At least 25 million kilometers of new power lines are needed over the next decade, with transmission build taking three to five times longer than data center construction itself.

NOAA/WMO Confirm Near-Record 'Super' El Niño Peaking Now

↑ Supply Deficit

NOAA's Climate Prediction Center and the WMO both rate the 2026-27 event a greater than 90% chance of becoming a very strong El Niño, peaking in November-December 2026 and persisting into early 2027 — potentially rivaling the historic 1997-98 Super El Niño. Raw sugar futures are up roughly 46% from their late-July low, cocoa futures jumped more than 50% earlier in the year, and Australian wheat sits in the highest exposure band for the 2026/27 harvest. The IMF estimates a typical El Niño lifts global food prices by about 5% within a year, with the 1997-98 event alone causing $5.7 trillion in cumulative global income losses.
