Central Banks Buy Record 288.9 Tonnes of Gold in Q2

↑ Structural Demand

The World Gold Council's Q2 2026 Gold Demand Trends report shows central banks purchased a net 288.9 tonnes of gold during the quarter, a 62% increase from 177.9 tonnes in Q2 2025 and the strongest second-quarter total on record, led by Poland, China, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan and the Czech Republic. The buying occurred even as gold recorded its steepest quarterly price decline since 2013, underscoring structural de-dollarization demand.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
