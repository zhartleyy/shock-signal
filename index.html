Methodology
How Catalyst Works
Four-step process from raw event to actionable signal
Step 01
📡
Scan
Monitor global news feeds for events that create sudden supply/demand imbalances — geopolitical shifts, policy changes, commodity disruptions, technology breakthroughs.
Step 02
🔗
Trace
Map causal chains from each event to specific publicly traded equities. Identify what gets scarcer or more abundant, and who wins or loses from that shift.
Step 03
🎯
Score
Assign conviction scores (1–10) based on directness of the causal link, the company's revenue exposure, and how much of the move is already priced into the stock.
Step 04
📊
Deliver
Package signals into a daily intelligence brief with macro context, double-beneficiary analysis, and individual signal cards for rapid consumption.
