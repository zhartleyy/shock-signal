Trade & Export ControlsRegulatory / Trade

China Extends Rare Earth Export Control Pause to January 2027

↑ Supply Normalization

China's Ministry of Commerce issued Announcement No. 58 on November 9, extending the suspension of six 2025 rare-earth export-control measures -- including the 0.1% extraterritorial rule and curbs on five additional elements -- from November 11, 2026 through January 10, 2027. The move de-escalates supply risk for magnet, battery and chip-material buyers even as Washington tightened AI chip export rules on November 16. China still controls roughly 70% of rare earth extraction and about 90% of processing capacity globally.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
