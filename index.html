How Catalyst Works
Step 01
Scan
Every morning, Catalyst ingests global news streams — geopolitical events, trade policy, commodity data, central bank moves, and regulatory changes.
Step 02
Trace
Each event is mapped to its supply or demand mechanism — what becomes scarcer or more abundant — and traced through the causal chain to specific publicly traded equities.
Step 03
Score
Signals are scored 1–10 based on three factors: directness of the causal link, revenue exposure of identified companies, and how much of the move is already priced in.
Step 04
Deliver
Winners and losers are surfaced daily before market open, grouped by macro theme, with double beneficiaries — stocks hit by multiple tailwinds — highlighted separately.
