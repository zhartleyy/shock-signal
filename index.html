Iran-US Peace Talks Crater Oil's War Premium

↓ Supply Normalization

Brent crude fell nearly 5% to $93.44 a barrel as Trump signaled progress on an Iran deal and Tehran's Supreme Leader granted the government powers to accelerate ceasefire talks, including demands to lift the US naval blockade and sanctions. DRAM Contract Prices Rocket 450% as AI Eats DDR5 Capacity

↓ Supply Deficit

16Gb DDR5 contract prices have surged from about $8.70 in October 2025 to roughly $48 by September 2026, a nearly 450% jump, as Samsung and SK Hynix redirect wafer capacity toward high-bandwidth memory for AI data centers. Team Group's CEO warned on a November 2 earnings call that the shortage will worsen from late 2026 through Q2 2027, with spot-to-contract spreads running 30-50% and supply/demand not balancing until as late as 2028. Gartner estimates average PC prices are up 17% year-over-year as a direct result.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
