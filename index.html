Fourth Drone Strike in Four Days Halts Kazakh Oil Exports via CPC

↓ Supply Disruption

Four tankers loading Kazakh crude at the Caspian Pipeline Consortium's Black Sea terminal near Novorossiysk have been struck by drones since July 19, forcing repeated suspensions of a route that carries over 1% of global oil supply and roughly 80% of Kazakhstan's crude exports. Kazakhstan has begun rerouting volumes through the Baku-Tbilisi-Ceyhan pipeline via Georgia and Turkey, bypassing Russia entirely. The disruptions compound Hormuz-related supply anxiety just as Middle East crude shipments have again nearly stopped.

European Heat Wave Slashes EU Corn Estimate by 3.7 MMT

↓ Supply Deficit

Record heat pushed temperatures above 100°F across France's grain belt for multiple weekends in a row, prompting USDA to cut its EU corn production estimate by 3.7 million tonnes to 53.78 mmt. Euronext corn futures have rallied more than 41 euros/tonne since mid-June on the damage, equal to roughly $1.19/bushel. The EU is now expected to ramp up corn imports to offset the shortfall, tightening global grain balances.

US Northern Plains Drought Depletes Soil Moisture as Wheat, Barley Enter Critical Stage

↓ Supply Risk

NASA soil moisture data show extremely below-normal conditions across the Dakotas, Nebraska, Minnesota and western Iowa as of July 22, following a long-duration heat wave with temperatures near or above 100°F. With 60% of the US barley crop and 54% of spring wheat having headed as of early July, the drought arrives at a critical vulnerability window for yield formation. Crop condition ratings have already begun declining across the region.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
