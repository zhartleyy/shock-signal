OPEC+ Output Hikes Turn Real as Hormuz Flows Normalize

↑ Supply Normalization

Total OPEC+ production dropped to 33.13 million bpd in May, down from 42.77 million bpd in February, as Iran's effective closure of the Strait of Hormuz throttled exports from Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Kuwait and the UAE. With that constraint now easing, analysts note quota increases that were previously a 'paper formality' are starting to show up as actual barrels, pressuring prices lower. This supply normalization favors refiners and consumers over high-cost producers.

↑ Cost-Push / Supply Chain Rerouting

Effective July 24, USTR imposed additional 10% or 12.5% tariffs on imports from 60 investigated economies, replacing expired temporary worldwide tariffs that had been in place since February. The action reshapes import cost structures across a huge swath of US trading partners, favoring domestic producers over import-reliant retailers.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
