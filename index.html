⚡ Energy / Geopolitics · Signal 01
Strait of Hormuz Blockade — Largest Oil Disruption in History
Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz on March 4 following US–Israeli airstrikes, cutting off ~20% of global oil supply. With 14 mb/d shut-in and cumulative losses exceeding 1 billion barrels, the IEA calls it the largest supply disruption ever recorded. Oil prices remain elevated near $110/bbl (North Sea Dated), having peaked at $144/bbl. Scarcity pricing flows directly to producers with unhedged exposure while crushing fuel-intensive industries.
