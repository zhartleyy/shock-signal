Houthi Threat to Bab el-Mandeb Compounds Hormuz Crisis

↑ Supply Disruption

Iran has instructed Yemen's Houthi forces to prepare to close the Bab el-Mandeb Strait if the US strikes Iran's power infrastructure, threatening the very Red Sea corridor Saudi Arabia built to route around Hormuz. Saudi Arabia has diverted over 70% of its normal crude exports to the Red Sea port of Yanbu since the war began, with Yanbu shipments averaging 4 million bpd versus roughly 973,000 bpd a year earlier. A simultaneous closure of both chokepoints would be unprecedented and could remove a double-digit share of seaborne crude supply overnight.

Section 122 Tariff Sunset Forces a Trade-Policy Scramble

Mixed Signal

The 10% Section 122 global tariff surcharge, imposed after courts struck down IEEPA-based tariffs, is set to expire July 24 unless Congress extends it, prompting the administration to reconstruct tariff walls via expanded Section 301 and Section 232 actions covering more than 75% of US imports. A new Section 301 forced-labor tariff of 10-12.5% is expected to replace the surcharge, while the postal de minimis duty-free threshold rises from $800 to $2,500. Separately, new 25% Section 301 tariffs on Brazilian goods take effect July 22, and Commerce has opened a circumvention inquiry into corrosion-resistant steel imports at the request of Nucor and Steel Dynamics.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.
