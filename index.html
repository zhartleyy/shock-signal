Iran Tensions Keep 8.3 mb/d of Gulf Oil Shut In

↓ Supply Deficit

Crude has posted back-to-back weekly gains of around 5%, with Brent near $87-95/bbl, as ship-to-ship transfers outside the Strait of Hormuz become standard practice amid continued attacks on tankers and infrastructure. Global oil supply is now forecast to decline 4.3 mb/d in 2026 to 102 mb/d, with 8.3 mb/d of Gulf output still shut in and regional exports down 2.1 mb/d after Hormuz was effectively closed again in early July. The disruption keeps a geopolitical risk premium embedded in prices even as the US reports helping tankers move 660 million barrels through the strait since May.

US Imposes 50% Tariffs on $20B of Canadian Goods

↑ Cost-Push Constraint

After three days of talks in Washington failed to produce a deal, the US imposed 50% tariffs on roughly $20-28 billion of Canadian exports effective August 22, hitting hockey sticks, wine, dairy, electronics and industrial machinery, layered on existing steel, aluminum and lumber duties. The escalation raises input costs for US importers of Canadian goods while shielding some domestic producers of competing products from Canadian competition.

Gold Tops $4,550 on Record Central Bank Buying

Volatile AI Loads Strain Data Center Equipment

Mixed Signal

Extreme swings in AI training and inference power draw are damaging data center infrastructure worldwide, with batteries needing replacement within weeks and gas turbine components cracking, including at xAI's Colossus facility in Memphis. Insufficient deployment of stabilizing equipment such as batteries, capacitors and flywheels is causing delays and curtailed operations at facilities across the US, Europe and the Middle East. The strain is creating a replacement and upgrade cycle for power-quality and grid-support equipment makers even as it raises operational risk for AI compute operators.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
