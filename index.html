Hormuz Transit Collapses to 5% of Pre-War Baseline

↓ Supply Deficit

Iran's IRGC insists the chokepoint, which normally carries roughly a fifth of global oil, remains under its full control, while CENTCOM disputes the claim citing continued flow. Tanker attacks have surged to a wartime high, with at least 12 strikes on oil, LNG and LPG vessels near the strait between Sept 28 and Oct 5 alone, and supertanker war-risk rates have hit $82 million.

China Extends Rare Earth Truce to January 2027

Mixed Signal

Following the September 2026 Washington summit, China extended its suspension of the sweeping October 2025 rare-earth export controls, covering holmium, erbium, thulium, europium and ytterbium, to January 10, 2027. However, the April 2025 licensing regime on seven other rare earths including dysprosium, terbium and yttrium remains fully in force, with the IEA warning up to $6.5 trillion of downstream production stays at risk if controls tighten again.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
