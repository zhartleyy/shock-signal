Hormuz FlashpointShipping / Insurance

War-Risk Premiums Hit 6-10% of Hull Value, Freight Economics Shift

↑ Cost-Push Constraint

War-risk insurance for a Hormuz transit now runs 6-10% of a tanker's hull value versus roughly 0.25% before the war, meaning a $100 million tanker pays $6-10 million per voyage against $250,000 previously. A 270,000-tonne VLCC transit can now cost about $21 million in insurance alone, with covers re-rated every 24 hours. The cost curtails willing capacity and lets owners pass the surcharge directly into freight.

Extraterritorial 0.1% Rule Threatens Chip and Defense Supply Chains

↓ Supply Constraint

The Category 2 rule requires a Chinese export license whenever Chinese-origin rare earth content reaches just 0.1% of a product's value, or when Chinese processing technology was used, directly touching semiconductor cerium-based wafer polishing and neodymium/dysprosium magnets used in HDD actuators and defense systems. Compliance teams at magnet, motor and defense suppliers face a licensing-queue risk that can stall a production line faster than a tariff.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
