Gulf of Fire: Dual Chokepoint CrisisEnergy / Geopolitical

Houthis Declare Maritime Embargo on Saudi Arabia, Squeeze Second Chokepoint

↓ Supply Deficit

Yemen's Houthis declared a maritime embargo on Saudi Arabia and deployed missiles and drones near Bab el-Mandeb, prompting several Saudi-cargo tankers to turn around before reaching the strait. Kpler data show Strait of Hormuz crossings fell 31% day-on-day to just nine vessels and Bab el-Mandeb crossings fell 34% to 29 on July 21. Saudi Arabia has rerouted crude via pipeline to its Red Sea Yanbu terminal, with exports there surging to 3.5 million barrels a day in June versus just 240,000 bpd a year earlier, as the dual chokepoint squeeze threatens roughly a fifth of global oil and LNG flows simultaneously.

Danube Hits Record Seasonal Lows as Heat Wave Kills Thousands, Threatens Harvests

↓ Supply Deficit

Prolonged drought and extreme heat across Europe pushed Danube river water levels to record seasonal lows as of July 22, with the heat wave killing thousands and drying out rivers, lakes, soils and crops while fueling destructive wildfires. The pattern echoes Europe's 2022 drought, when low water levels curtailed hydropower generation, forced nuclear cooling constraints, and cut corn harvests by nearly 19%, raising the risk of a similar hit to this year's grain and power supply.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
