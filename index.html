Russia's Refinery WarEnergy / Geopolitical

Ukraine's 21st August Refinery Strike Cripples Russian Fuel Output

↓ Supply Deficit

Ukraine struck the Yanos refinery in Yaroslavl, which can process up to 300,000 barrels of oil per day, on August 28 - the 21st attack on Russian refineries this month alone, more than any month since the war began - following earlier strikes on the Afipsky plant and Lukoil's NORSI refinery. The sustained campaign has pushed Russian oil-processing rates to multiyear lows, forcing Moscow into fuel rationing and a temporary export ban on diesel, gasoline and jet fuel. Reduced Russian refined-product exports are tightening global distillate markets and lifting refining margins elsewhere.

Russia's Refinery WarEnergy / Regulatory

Moscow's Export Ban on Diesel and Gasoline Tightens Global Diesel Market

↑ Demand Surge

To keep fuel flowing domestically amid the refinery attacks and resulting shortages and rationing, Russia imposed a temporary ban on most exports of diesel, gasoline and jet fuel. Russia has historically supplied a meaningful share of seaborne diesel to global markets, so the ban forces buyers in Africa, Latin America and the Mediterranean to source from Gulf Coast and Asian refiners, supporting distillate crack spreads even as crude itself eases.

Copper Squeeze Deepens as AI Data Centers Collide With Mine-Supply Deficit

↓ Supply Deficit

Copper output from top producer Chile fell 9% year-on-year in March, with Codelco down 10% and BHP's Escondida down nearly 16%, while forecasters now see a roughly 600,000-tonne refined copper deficit in 2026 versus 200,000 tonnes in 2025. AI data-center buildout and grid/EV electrification are adding structural demand just as new mine supply growth slows to about 1.4% a year, keeping prices near record levels.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.
