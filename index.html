China Extends Rare-Earth Export Control Suspension to Jan 10, 2027

↑ Supply Normalization

China's Ministry of Commerce extended the suspension of its sweeping October 2025 rare-earth export controls, covering magnets, batteries and graphite anodes, from Nov 11, 2026 through Jan 10, 2027, matching a parallel US move to extend tariff exclusions on 178 Chinese products and pause Section 301 port fees on Chinese vessels. The de-escalation removes near-term licensing risk for magnet-dependent supply chains in autos, electronics and defense, but also reduces urgency and pricing power for companies building ex-China rare-earth alternatives.

European Gas Jumps 8.6% as Cold Snap Threatens Winter Supplies

↑ Demand Surge

European benchmark natural gas futures surged as much as 8.6% to €96.075/MWh, the biggest intraday gain in more than a week, as updated forecasts showed colder conditions persisting across northwest Europe into early December rather than the brief chill previously expected. The repricing compounds existing supply pressure from Iran-war-linked export disruptions and follows a rare, unusually early polar vortex disruption around Nov 10-13 that raises the odds of a broader Northern Hemisphere cold outbreak. Tight winter inventories leave Europe exposed to further price spikes if the cold persists.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
