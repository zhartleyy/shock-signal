European Heat & Drought Crop ShockAgriculture / Climate

European Heatwave Wipes Out €1.8-2.1 Billion in Grain Crops

↓ Supply Deficit

The June heat dome cut EU and UK grain production forecasts by nearly 9 million metric tons, worth an estimated €2.1 billion in national wheat, barley and corn prices, or about 5% of 2025 production value. France accounts for nearly half the losses with its harvest forecast cut by 4.1 million tons (~€891 million) and is heading for its worst maize harvest in 50 years, while Romania has lost more than a million hectares of crops. Temperatures during the critical grain-filling period ran 3°C above the 1991-2020 average across France, Germany, Austria, Poland and Hungary.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
