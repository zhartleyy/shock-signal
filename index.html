IRGC Mines Three Tankers as Hormuz Attacks Hit War-High

Mixed Signal

The IRGC said three oil tankers hit naval mines and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz on Nov 23, part of a wave that has brought reported attacks to their highest monthly level since the Iran war began in February, with at least 22 incidents in November. Shipping through the strait remains roughly 65% below pre-war levels and about half of transiting vessels are energy carriers, raising war-risk insurance and rerouting costs. Despite the escalation, Brent still fell as traders focused on a broader supply glut, showing geopolitical risk premiums are being offset by non-OPEC flows.

COP31 Ends in Disarray With No Fossil-Fuel Phase-Out Language

↓ Regulatory Pressure

The outcome removes a near-term binding catalyst for accelerated decarbonization commitments heading into 2027.

European Winter Gas SqueezeEnergy / Climatic

TTF Gas Spikes 8.6% as Cold Snap Meets Hormuz-Driven LNG Shortfall

↑ Demand Surge

Dutch TTF futures jumped 8.6% to about €96/MWh on Nov 23 as forecasts showed temperatures 3-8°C below normal across northwest Europe into early December, compounding a reserve base that is already running thin after disrupted Middle Eastern LNG exports from the Iran war. Weaker wind output is forcing greater reliance on gas-fired power, with German December power up as much as 6.7% and January power up 6%. The combination of a structural LNG deficit and a weather-driven demand spike is pulling capital toward alternative baseload and export infrastructure.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
