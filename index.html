methodology
How Catalyst Works
Step 01 — Scan

Global Event Monitoring

Every morning, Catalyst scans hundreds of news sources, regulatory filings, and intelligence feeds for events with the potential to create supply or demand imbalances across global commodity and equity markets.

Step 02 — Trace

Causal Chain Mapping

Each event is traced through its mechanism: what gets scarcer or more abundant? Which companies have direct revenue or cost exposure? How does the shock propagate through supply chains, competitors, and end markets?

Step 03 — Score

Conviction Scoring

Signals are scored 1-10 based on three factors: directness of causal link (how many steps to impact), magnitude of revenue exposure, and how much is already priced in by the market. High scores require all three to align.

Step 04 — Deliver

Daily Intelligence Brief

Signals are surfaced daily in this dashboard, updated before market open. Double beneficiaries — stocks positively impacted by two or more independent signals — are highlighted as potentially higher-conviction opportunities.
