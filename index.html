US Closes Remote AI Chip Access Loophole

↓ Supply Constraint

The Commerce Department's BIS issued an interim rule on Nov 16 requiring export licenses for AI chips and servers tied to data centers anywhere in the world that supply over 12 million TPP of compute to China-linked entities, explicitly naming Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia and Brazil as higher-risk jurisdictions. The rule also formally rescinds the Biden-era AI Diffusion Rule. It arrives just three days before Trump is set to meet Xi Jinping.

VLCC Freight Rates Crash 64% From October Peak as Middle East Exports Flood Market

↓ Demand Normalization

Gulf of Oman-to-China VLCC freight rates fell to an index reading of 308 on Nov 13, down 64% from the October 8 peak of 841, as surging Middle East crude exports shorten average voyage distances and swell available tonnage. The reversal comes just a quarter after Frontline had booked 86% of its Q3 VLCC days at nearly $157,000 per day. Fewer long-haul voyages from the US and West Africa are now needed as Gulf barrels recapture share.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.
