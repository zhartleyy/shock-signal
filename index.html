Global Diesel CrunchEnergy / Refining

Russia Extends Diesel Export Ban Through October as Refinery Strikes Cripple Capacity

↓ Supply Deficit

Russia extended its ban on diesel, marine fuel and gasoil exports by producers through October 31 after Ukrainian drone strikes knocked three of its six largest diesel refineries—Kirishi, Volgograd and NORSI—offline or down to roughly 25% capacity. Ukraine claims its campaign has disabled more than 45% of Russia's total refining capacity, pushing US diesel to a record $6.27 a gallon. Russian diesel exports fell below 1 million metric tons in June versus roughly 2.5 million tons a year earlier, forcing buyers like Turkey and Brazil to lose at least half their prior cargo allotments.

China Halts October Fuel Exports, Removing Critical Swing Supply

↓ Supply Deficit

China's major refiners suspended most refined-fuel exports for October outside Hong Kong and Macau, with PetroChina cancelling several gasoline and jet-fuel cargoes, as Beijing prioritizes rebuilding domestic inventories that Kpler estimates are roughly 20 million barrels below pre-war diesel levels. The halt comes a week after Trump personally pressed Xi Jinping to boost refined-product exports to stabilize global supply, and instead triggered a 4.6% Brent surge above $100/bbl. August exports had hit 6.01 million tonnes, up 12.7% year-on-year, making the reversal especially disruptive to Asian product markets.

Meta Faces Up to $40B Penalty in New Mexico Privacy Verdict

Rate Constraint

A ruling is expected later this month, and the size of any award could set precedent for similar state-level privacy litigation against other platforms.

Methodology

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.
