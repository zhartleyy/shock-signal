Trump Announces 100-Year Venezuela Oil Concession Covering 65 Billion Barrels

Mixed Signal

Trump said the US has entered a sweeping agreement giving an American company 55% effective output and an ownership stake in a new entity controlling Venezuela's untapped reserves, which would make it the second-largest corporate holder of proven reserves after Saudi Aramco. Crude prices actually eased on the initial announcement because it signals a large future supply release, even as the Hormuz escalation pushed prices back up days later. Venezuelan heavy crude export volumes were already at a seven-year high before the deal was announced.

US-Canada Tariff War Deepens: 50% Duties Meet Retaliation on 700+ Products

↓ Trade Flow Disruption

The US imposed 50% tariffs on roughly $20 billion of Canadian goods, about 5% of Canadian exports, after trade talks collapsed, adding to existing duties on steel, aluminum, lumber and autos. Canada confirmed retaliatory tariffs of 15%, 25% or 50% on more than 700 US-origin products, including dairy, appliances, paper products and construction materials, set to take effect September 8. The escalation threatens cross-border automotive and agricultural supply chains on both sides of the border.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
