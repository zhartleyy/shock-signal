Saudi East-West Pipeline Attack Cuts 4-5% of Global Oil Supply

↓ Supply Shock

Drone strikes launched from Iraqi territory hit pumping stations on Saudi Arabia's 1,200km East-West pipeline on September 10-11, forcing a precautionary shutdown of a route that had been rerouting 4-5 million barrels per day around the blockaded Strait of Hormuz. Repairs could take five to six weeks according to sources close to Reuters, and Brent spiked to $108.83 intraday before easing on reports the pipeline was ramping back up. The outage compounds an already-disrupted Hormuz corridor that historically carried 25% of global seaborne oil trade.

Hormuz Traffic Rebounds to 80% of Pre-War Volume

The partial normalization is capping the risk premium embedded in Brent even though war-risk insurance and tanker rates remain elevated.

Houthi Advance Threatens Second Chokepoint at Bab el-Mandeb

↓ Supply Risk

Iran-backed Houthi forces have captured strategic points along the Red Sea and launched a ground offensive toward controlling the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, a critical energy and shipping route on the other side of the Arabian Peninsula from Hormuz. The Houthis declared a maritime embargo against Saudi Arabia in July and have escalated missile and drone strikes on Riyadh and Khamis Mushait. A second chokepoint disruption would compound an already-fragile global fuel picture and force costly rerouting of tankers and container ships.

Texas Statewide Diesel Disaster Declaration

↑ Demand Surge

Refiners including Valero, Marathon Petroleum, HF Sinclair and Phillips 66 have logged six straight weeks of gains on record crack spreads.

Washington Weighs Diesel Export Ban

Mixed Signal

The US Energy Secretary has sought refiners' help to curb diesel prices, and Republican leaders are floating an outright export ban as domestic prices spiral, a policy shift estimated to be worth $321 million a day in cost relief for domestic buyers versus 2025. Valero shares fell 4.1% on the mere signal of policy review, with Marathon Petroleum and Phillips 66 also selling off on regulatory ambiguity. European gasoil futures already trade at a $78-per-metric-ton premium to US Gulf Coast benchmarks, showing how binding an export restriction could be.

Fed Raises Rates to 3.75%-4.00%, Signals One More Hike

Rate Constraint

The FOMC voted 12-0 on September 16 to raise the federal funds rate 25 basis points to 3.75%-4.00%, the first hike in more than three years, citing inflation kept elevated by spiraling oil prices and core PCE running above 3% every month of 2026. The September dot plot shows the median official expecting one more 25bp hike before year-end, with fed funds futures pricing 4.2% by December. Chair Kevin Warsh emphasized the Fed is not yet confident inflation is moving to target at sufficient speed.

Commerce Shifts China Fab Equipment to Annual License Regime

Regulatory Constraint

US authorities have moved Samsung Electronics, SK Hynix and TSMC from blanket export waivers to year-by-year license approvals for shipping American chipmaking tools into their Chinese fabrication plants, adding planning uncertainty to memory and logic supply chains. Separately, Chinese customs has told agents that Nvidia's H200 chips are not permitted entry without explanation, while officials have warned domestic firms against buying them unless necessary. The dueling restrictions are accelerating Beijing's push to force domestic chip-consuming firms toward indigenous alternatives.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
