Hormuz Traffic Craters to 25 Ships/Day as War-Risk Insurance Hits 10% of Hull Value

China Slaps Immediate Ban on Helium Exports, Rattling Global Chip Supply Chain

↓ Supply Constraint

China's Commerce Ministry and Customs agency imposed an open-ended, immediate helium export ban on July 10, citing risks from the reignited Iran war. China imports more than 85% of its helium, with Qatar historically supplying over half, and Qatari output has been crippled by strikes on Ras Laffan facilities since the Hormuz crisis began. Spot helium prices in Northeast Asia have spiked to $150-205 per thousand cubic feet, roughly double late-2025 levels, tightening a critical input for semiconductor wafer cooling, MRI machines and aerospace.

Tariff Wall 2.0Trade / Autos & Consumer

Trump Imposes 50% Tariff on Canadian Autos, Alcohol, Dairy and Furniture

↑ Cost Shock

President Trump signed three proclamations under the Depression-era Section 338 statute imposing an additional 50% ad valorem duty on Canadian autos, alcohol, dairy, furniture and other categories, effective August 19, 2026. The order excludes energy, potash, Section 232-covered goods, fish and critical minerals, but applies broadly to consumer and auto categories, with no exemption for goods already in transit. Automakers with heavy Ontario assembly exposure face the steepest cost increases on cross-border shipments.

USTR Rolls Out New 10-12.5% Tariffs on 60 Trading Partners as Section 122 Expires

↑ Cost Shock

The U.S. trade representative replaced the expired 10% Section 122 tariff with new Section 301 forced-labor duties of 10-12.5% on 60 trading partners effective July 24, 2026, covering nearly all major U.S. trading relationships outside existing carve-outs. The new duties do not stack on top of Section 232 tariffs, unlike China's existing Section 301 regime, but they raise landed costs for import-dependent retailers and consumer goods companies broadly.

Brazil Hit With 25% Section 301 Tariff on Exports to the U.S.

↑ Cost Shock

The U.S. announced a new 25% Section 301 tariff on Brazilian goods effective July 22, 2026, with an exemption only for cargo loaded before the 22nd and entered before July 29. The tariff does not stack on Section 232 duties and comes with an expanded exemption annex, but it raises costs across Brazilian coffee, orange juice, beef and aerospace exports to the U.S., squeezing companies reliant on Brazilian sourcing or export sales.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
