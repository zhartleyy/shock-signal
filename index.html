US Plains Heat Wave Threatens Wheat and Barley

↓ Supply Risk

Temperatures hit 115°F in Montana and set daily records in Bismarck and Dickinson, North Dakota, arriving just as USDA data showed 60% of the barley crop and 54% of spring wheat had already headed, a critical and heat-vulnerable growth stage. Soil moisture is deteriorating rapidly from Wyoming through the Dakotas into Minnesota and Iowa, and the ten-day rainfall outlook offers only isolated relief, raising the risk of a meaningful yield hit into the WASDE updates.
