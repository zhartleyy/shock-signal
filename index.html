Trump Imposes 50% Section 338 Tariffs on Canadian Autos, Dairy and Lumber

↑ Cost Push

Trump signed three proclamations under the rarely used Section 338 imposing a 50% tariff on a wide swath of Canadian goods - including autos, dairy, cement, wine and lumber - effective August 19, with energy, potash and critical minerals explicitly carved out. The tariffs override USMCA-compliance protections entirely, marking a structural break from the 2020 trade pact and threatening Canada's integrated automotive and lumber supply chains.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
