WTI Surges to One-Month High as US-Iran Ceasefire Collapses

↓ Supply Deficit

WTI crude jumped roughly 16% last week to close near $82.49/bbl after the US reimposed a naval blockade on Iran and launched fresh strikes, while Iran retaliated against US bases in Bahrain, Jordan, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar and Syria. Tanker traffic through the Strait of Hormuz, which carries about 20% of global oil shipments, fell to two-month lows, with just seven vessels transiting the day after the blockade versus 13 the day before. A drone strike also suspended crude loadings at Iraq's Basra export terminals, compounding the supply shock. The IEA warns prolonged disruption could threaten global energy security.

VLCC Tanker Rates Rocket as War-Risk Insurers Pull Cover

↑ Freight Rate Shock

Jet Fuel Crack Spread Hits Record, Halving Airline Profitability

↑ Cost Shock

IATA now expects global airline net profit to shrink from $45B in 2025 to just $23B in 2026, with margins falling from 4.2% to 2.0%, as jet fuel prices are set to average $152/bbl, up nearly 70% year-on-year, and the crack spread over Brent hits a historic high of $57/bbl. US carriers alone face an estimated extra $11B in 2026 fuel costs, and Delta has flagged a $2B quarterly fuel cost hit despite owning its own refinery. Airlines are cutting routes, deferring aircraft deliveries, and raising baggage fees and fares by up to 20% to offset the hit.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
