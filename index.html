Energy / OPEC
UAE Exits OPEC — Cartel Spare Capacity Craters from 3.8M to 2.5M bpd
Effective May 1, the UAE became the third-largest OPEC producer to exit the cartel, removing its 3.7M bpd production and — critically — its spare capacity buffer. OPEC's ability to respond to supply shocks is now almost entirely concentrated in Saudi Arabia, reducing the cartel's market share from ~30% to ~26%. With the Hormuz crisis already straining global inventories, this structural change removes the market's last reliable shock absorber and puts a structural floor under oil prices.

Critical Minerals / Trade
China Rare Earth Export Restrictions Persist — EV & Solar Supply Chains Under Pressure
Despite the trade truce, China's rare earth export restrictions — imposed in retaliation for the H20 chip ban — remain in effect. China controls 60%+ of rare earth mining and 85%+ of processing. The restrictions are targeting neodymium (permanent magnets in EVs and wind turbines), dysprosium, and terbium. US and European EV manufacturers face BOM cost increases of $400–800 per vehicle in the near term. Solar panel manufacturers similarly face higher costs for rare earth-containing components. The truce creates uncertainty but removal of restrictions was not confirmed.

Methodology

How Catalyst Works

01Scan
Global Event Scan
Every morning, Catalyst monitors global news across geopolitics, trade policy, energy markets, technology shifts, financial stress events, natural disasters, and regulatory changes — looking for events that create real supply or demand shocks.
02Trace
Causal Chain Mapping
For each event, Catalyst traces the mechanism: what gets scarcer or more abundant? Who controls that resource? Which publicly traded companies have material revenue or cost exposure? Winners and losers are identified by following the economic chain, not headlines.
03Score
Conviction Scoring
Each signal is scored 1–10 based on three factors: (1) directness of the causal link, (2) magnitude of revenue or cost exposure, and (3) how much is already priced in. High conviction signals combine all three. Scores are not buy/sell ratings.
04Deliver
Daily Brief Generation
Catalyst compiles every signal into a structured, searchable daily brief — identifying macro themes, double beneficiaries (stocks hit by 2+ signals), and individual signal cards with full analysis and conviction-scored stock tables.
