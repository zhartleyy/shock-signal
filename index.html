Ukrainian Drone Campaign Cripples Russian Refining to 24-Year Low

↓ Supply Deficit

The damage has cut petrol/diesel availability to just 28.1% of Russian filling stations, down from 41% a week earlier, forcing Moscow to extend export bans and import fuel via Murmansk. The resulting hole in global diesel supply is tightening crack spreads worldwide and rerouting tanker trade.

China Tightens Rare-Earth Licensing, Extraterritorial Rules Loom

↓ Supply Deficit

China has expanded licensing requirements on rare-earth compounds including samarium, gadolinium and lutetium, with extraterritorial enforcement covering foreign-made products containing Chinese-sourced material delayed until November 2026. China accounts for 61% of global mined rare-earth supply and 91% of refining capacity, giving Beijing significant leverage over defense, EV and electronics supply chains. The compliance window is prompting a scramble to build non-China sourcing.

Critical Minerals SqueezeIndustrial / Commodities

Copper Concentrate Crisis Deepens as Smelters Pay Negative Fees

↓ Supply Deficit

Copper treatment charges have fallen to roughly -$66/tonne, meaning smelters now pay miners for concentrate access rather than being paid to process it, as mine output from Chile's Codelco and Escondida has fallen double digits year-on-year. The ICSG flipped its 2026 forecast from a 209,000-tonne surplus to a deficit exceeding 150,000 tonnes within six months, while some banks now see deficits as large as 600,000 tonnes. Prices near record highs above $12,000/tonne reflect a structural supply-demand mismatch rather than speculation.

Hyperscalers are responding by signing direct deals with generators and pursuing on-site power rather than waiting on grid interconnection queues that now run 4-10 years.
