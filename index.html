Trump Rejects Iran's Hormuz Reopening Plan, Brent Jumps Above $106

↑ Supply Disruption

President Trump rejected Iran's seven-day proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, saying Tehran had overplayed its hand, while Iran said it would not soften its conditions. Brent crude climbed as high as $108.83 intraday before settling near $106.89, reigniting a crisis that has disrupted roughly 25% of the world's seaborne oil trade and 19-20% of LNG flows through the chokepoint since February. The renewed tension pushed October Fed rate-hike odds from 64.2% to 70.3% overnight and knocked gold down 3% in sympathy with inflation fears.

Saudi Aramco Partially Restarts East-West Pipeline, Easing Supply Squeeze

↓ Supply Normalization

Saudi Arabia restarted its East-West pipeline on September 22 after a nine-day shutdown from drone strikes, working to restore the roughly 4 million bpd it had been rerouting to the Red Sea port of Yanbu since the Hormuz crisis began. Brent fell more than $2 a barrel on the news to its lowest level since September 8, though a security source said full restoration of all damaged pump stations could take several more weeks. Saudi Arabia also loaded seven supertankers carrying 14 million barrels directly through Hormuz over the weekend, the clearest sign yet of exports shifting back to the strait.

Tariff Cliffs: September 29 DeadlinesRegulatory / Pharma

100% Pharma Tariff Cliff Hits Non-Annex III Drugmakers

↑ Cost Shock

A 100% Section 232 tariff on patented pharmaceuticals, biologics, and their active ingredients takes effect September 29 for all companies not listed in Annex III of the April proclamation or without a Commerce-approved onshoring plan or MFN pricing agreement; the 17 largest pharma companies already faced this rate starting July 31. Generic pharmaceuticals, biosimilars, and orphan drugs remain exempt, and preferential rates apply to imports from Japan, the EU, Korea, Switzerland, Liechtenstein and the UK. Smaller branded manufacturers with imported API supply chains face an immediate doubling of landed drug costs.

US Import Ban Takes Effect on Select Canadian Goods

↑ Supply Constraint

Beginning 12:01 a.m. ET on September 29, certain Canadian products are prohibited from entering the United States, with kitchen cabinets and vanities facing a separate 25% tariff and UK imports capped at 10% duty. The action follows Canada's own retaliatory tariffs of 15%, 25%, or 50% on more than 700 US-origin products that took effect September 8, including a doubling of Canada's steel and aluminum counter-tariff to 50%. A decision on additional duties for hardwood lumber and timber imports is due by October 1.

BIS Tightens Entity List Affiliate Rule, Squeezing China Compute Access

Mixed Signal

The Bureau of Industry and Security's 50% Affiliates Rule automatically extends export restrictions to any foreign company 50% or more owned by an Entity-Listed firm, but Aivres reportedly held just a 33% stake structure that kept it below the threshold. Subsequent May 2026 guidance requires export licenses for any company whose ultimate parent is headquartered in an arms-embargoed country or Macau, even if the company itself sits elsewhere, closing part of the loophole. Nvidia's China data-center compute market share has reportedly collapsed toward zero on new H200 shipments amid the enforcement ambiguity, even as overall global AI chip demand remains robust.

The season has now tied the record of three Category 5 hurricanes in a single Pacific season.

Historically Quiet Atlantic Season Lowers Gulf Coast Risk Premium

↓ Risk Premium

The 2026 Atlantic season surpassed the latest date for a first hurricane in the satellite era on September 11, remaining extremely inactive due to a very strong El Nino event in the Pacific. The combined accumulated cyclone energy index across seven global basins stood at just 617.7 units as of September 25, well below the seasonal pace needed to threaten Gulf Coast energy infrastructure. The absence of storm-driven shut-ins has kept refining runs and offshore production steady into the fourth quarter.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.
