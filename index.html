Methodology

How Catalyst works

A four-step pipeline, repeated each morning before market open.

01 ·

Scan

Sweep the last 24h of global news for events capable of shifting supply or demand for a traded asset — geopolitics, trade policy, energy, tech, credit, weather, regulation.

02 ·

Trace

For each event, write the causal chain explicitly: what gets scarcer or more abundant, which industries feel it first, which companies sit in that pipe.

03 ·

Score

Assign a 1–10 conviction score based on directness of the link, revenue exposure, and how much of the move is already in the stock.

04 ·

Deliver

Publish a clean brief with winners, losers, tickers, theses, and scores. No hype, no jargon, no "buy signals" — just the chain.
