Strait of Hormuz Blockade Reaches Day 208 as War-Risk Insurance Hits 40x

↑ Supply Disruption / Freight Cost

The Strait of Hormuz remains effectively closed to commercial shipping more than six months after the 2026 US-Israeli strikes on Iran, with war-risk insurance quoted at roughly 40 times peacetime rates and multiple P&I clubs withdrawing cover entirely. Four of the nine largest container carriers have stopped using the strait, with at least 41 vessels and 204,000 TEU stranded inside the Gulf. The blockage has removed roughly a quarter of the world's seaborne oil trade and a fifth of LNG shipments from normal routing.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
