Middle East Oil Supply ShockEnergy / Geopolitical

Houthi Strikes on Saudi Arabia, Fresh Tanker Attacks Lift Brent

↑ Supply Risk Premium

Yemen's Iran-backed Houthis escalated strikes on Saudi Arabia's Riyadh airport while two tankers were attacked over the weekend near Oman and in the Strait of Hormuz, pushing Brent up about 2% to roughly $105-106/bbl, a two-week high. Eleven tankers were attacked in Hormuz in the week to October 4, the highest weekly total since the war began, threatening the fragile rebound in Middle East crude exports seen in September. Goldman Sachs raised its December Brent forecast to $90/bbl from $85, estimating a risk premium of just over $20/bbl.

Diesel Scarcity & Refiner WindfallEnergy / Refining

Diesel Crack Spread Holds Near $100/bbl as a Third of Exports Stay Offline

↑ Margin Expansion

Roughly one-third of global diesel exports remain curtailed by Russian refinery damage (capacity cut by as much as 60%) and reduced Middle East refinery runs (down 2.0 million b/d), keeping the NY Harbor ULSD crack spread near $101.71/bbl in early October after hitting a record $117.92/bbl on September 16. The 3-2-1 crack spread stood near $64.83/bbl on October 6, close to the widest level in a data series running back to 1986. Marathon Petroleum, Valero and Phillips 66 posted a combined $12.6 billion in quarterly profit, their strongest result since 2022.

Record $6.50+ Diesel Squeezes Truckers and Airlines

↓ Margin Compression (end users)

US retail diesel hit an all-time high of $6.51 per gallon, driven by refinery capacity constraints rather than a lack of crude, since US refineries are already running near 96% of capacity. The EIA expects distillate inventories to stay below the five-year range through much of 2027, with stocks potentially falling below 100 million barrels for the first time in over two decades. Washington is debating a diesel export ban to ease domestic prices, a move opponents warn could worsen the global shortage.

EU Storage Lags Prior Year by 9 Points as TTF Jumps 8% in a Week

↑ Supply Deficit

US LNG Exporters Capture Transatlantic Arbitrage

↑ Demand Surge

With the Henry Hub-TTF spread exceeding $20 per MMBtu in early October and Asian JKM near $25.98/MMBtu, US LNG exporters are running terminals at full utilization to capture both European and Asian premiums simultaneously. ENTSOG warned Europe's gas system can meet winter needs but faces bottleneck risk if particularly strenuous weather conditions emerge, underscoring sustained demand for flexible US cargoes. The competition between Asian and European buyers for the same marginal LNG supply is expected to keep US export economics favorable through the winter heating season.

Rare Earth Supply Chain EndgameMaterials / Defense

China's Nov. 10 Export-Control Deadline Forces US Reshoring Push

↑ Supply Risk / Reshoring Premium

China's one-year suspension of its sweeping October 2025 rare-earth export controls, including extraterritorial licensing rules affecting foreign-made products containing trace Chinese-origin rare earths, expires November 10, 2026. China already added MP Materials and USA Rare Earth to its own export control list in June 2026, and the IEA has estimated roughly $6.5 trillion of downstream production outside China is at risk under full enforcement. Congress is fast-tracking four bills covering mine permitting, a $2.5 billion strategic reserve, and defense supply chain protections ahead of the deadline.

How Catalyst Works

Methodology
STEP 01 🔍

Scan

Every morning, Catalyst scans global news for events creating supply or demand shocks — geopolitical, financial, regulatory, climatic and technological.

STEP 02 🔗

Trace

Each event is traced through its causal chain: what becomes scarcer or more abundant, and which companies sit directly in the path of that change?

STEP 03 🎯

Score

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

STEP 04 📬

Deliver

The full brief is published each morning with winners, losers, macro themes and double beneficiaries — ready before markets open.
