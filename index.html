Nvidia Board Ousts Jensen Huang Amid Espionage Probe

Mixed Signal

Nvidia's board accepted founder Jensen Huang's resignation as President, CEO and director effective Nov. 11, naming CFO Colette Kress interim CEO after an investigation into Huang's personal relationships, including one involving a woman reportedly linked to Chinese intelligence. The shakeup has accelerated customer diversification: Nvidia's share of contracted AI compute at frontier labs has fallen to 39% from an 83% chip-revenue share as Google, Amazon and Microsoft shift workloads to Broadcom custom silicon, Google TPUs and AMD GPUs. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has publicly urged the board to hire an outside CEO, adding to leadership uncertainty heading into Nvidia's Nov. 17 earnings report.

Iran Claims Six Tankers Mined in Hormuz in 48 Hours

↑ Supply Risk Premium

Iran's navy and Revolutionary Guard reported a total of six oil tankers struck naval mines and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz over Sunday and Monday, with a seventh hit confirmed by Iran's army Monday night, part of a campaign Tehran says targets vessels using unauthorized routes. The U.S. military maintains it has cleared the main shipping lanes, but Brent crude still rose 0.3% to $96.13 a barrel Monday on the renewed incidents, even as prices remain down roughly 13% over the past month. The escalation revives the risk premium just as global buyers increasingly hedge Middle East exposure by diversifying toward U.S. LNG and non-Gulf crude.

Gulf Freight Premium Persists Even as Non-Gulf Rates Crash

Mixed Signal

Baltic Exchange data show VLCC Worldscale rates from the Gulf of Oman to China collapsing 64% to 308 points by Nov. 13 from October's record 841, as Gulf exporters restored flows to near pre-war levels using ship-to-ship transfers and dark-fleet tactics. Yet a Persian Gulf-to-China VLCC charter still costs roughly $72-74 million (about $36-37 per barrel), some $20 per barrel more expensive than a voyage originating just outside the strait in the Gulf of Oman. DHT has already booked 68% of its fourth-quarter spot VLCC days at $579,900 a day, even though tanker-owner share prices imply the market expects average 2027 rates near just $55,000 a day.

Fed Hawkish PivotFinancial / Monetary

Markets Price 96% Odds of a December Rate Hike

Rate Constraint

Kalshi and Polymarket traders now assign roughly 96% probability to a quarter-point hike at the Fed's Dec. 9 FOMC meeting, with 87-92% favoring exactly 25 basis points, a dramatic reversal from earlier-year expectations of cuts. The fed funds target has held at 3.75%-4.00% since the Oct. 28 meeting, and traders see little on the calendar capable of derailing a hike given tariff- and energy-driven price pressure still working through the economy. Higher-for-longer policy supports net interest margins at large banks and money-market yields while squeezing rate-sensitive sectors like homebuilders and REITs.

GE Vernova's Gas Turbine Backlog Hits $197 Billion

↑ Demand Surge

GE Vernova's total order backlog reached $197 billion in its latest quarter, including $104 billion of equipment backlog up 92% year over year, as data-center operators compete with utilities for scarce gas-turbine capacity. The company now counts roughly 100 gas-power customers across 25 countries, of which 20% are data centers, and 2026 gas-equipment pricing per kilowatt is running almost 50% above 2025 levels. Turbine delivery slots are being booked into the 2030s, with GE Vernova targeting 20 gigawatts of annualized output in 2026 rising to 30 gigawatts by 2030.

Venture Global Links LNG Expansion to 10GW of AI Compute

↑ Demand Surge

Venture Global announced multi-year frame agreements with GE Vernova and Baker Hughes for up to 16 new 720-megawatt gas-fired power islands, designed to supply behind-the-meter electricity for LNG facility expansion and potentially up to 10 gigawatts of future AI compute without drawing on public grids. The deal ties LNG export growth directly to data-center power economics, reinforcing gas turbines and grid equipment as the hard physical constraint on AI buildouts. It follows a broader pattern in which developers push toward on-site gas generation, with BloombergNEF tracking 181 gigawatts of announced gas-fired capacity tied to data centers, over 90% of it in the U.S.

US Container Imports Break a Four-Year Volume Record

↑ Demand Surge

U.S. container imports broke a four-year volume record in October as retailers front-loaded inventory ahead of tariff deadlines and the holiday season, according to Global Port Tracker data cited by Supply Chain 24/7. STG Logistics' CEO says the restocking rush is straining truck and rail capacity, with truckers increasingly passing on loads as networks tighten. The surge compounds existing congestion at Asian hubs including Singapore, Port Klang, Shanghai and Ningbo, where container shipping capacity on East-West lanes reached unprecedented levels in October and November.

USPS Makes Fuel Surcharge Permanent, Hikes Parcel Prices 5.9%

↑ Cost Pass-Through

USPS plans to raise parcel prices 5.9% in January 2027 and lock in its fuel surcharge permanently at 11%, pending regulatory review, a move that resets the pricing umbrella for the broader parcel and freight market. Private carriers and truckload/intermodal operators that already pass through fuel costs gain room to raise rates alongside the postal service's increase without losing competitive position. The move lands as UPS simultaneously sues the U.S. government to recover tariff refunds owed on entries that Customs could not process through its automated system.

DRAM Shortage Deepens as AI Servers Absorb Memory Supply

↓ Supply Deficit

DRAM remains the highest supply-chain risk heading into 2027 as AI infrastructure and server demand receive priority allocation from memory manufacturers, squeezing availability for conventional DDR3, DDR4 and DDR5 buyers, according to a November supply-chain brief. Large memory customers are increasingly locking in future production capacity years in advance, and the same report flags emerging shortages in PCB glass-fabric materials, including the 1080, 106 and 2116 constructions, as multiple CCL suppliers operate under allocation. The tightening is extending lead times and pricing power to memory suppliers just as AI data-center buildouts accelerate demand further.
