Russia's Refining CollapseEnergy / Geopolitical

Russian Refining Falls to 24-Year Low After Drone Campaign

↓ Supply Deficit

Bloomberg reports Russian refining fell to just 3.6 million bpd in July, the lowest level since May 2002 and roughly one-third below seasonal norms, as Ukrainian drone strikes intensified against refineries, tankers, and export terminals. Diesel and gasoil exports crashed to only 80,000 bpd in the first week of August, down from more than 1 million bpd at the end of last year. Nearly a quarter of Russia's total refining capacity is now offline or running at reduced rates, tightening the global distillate market and lifting margins for refiners outside Russia.

Treasury Debasement TradeFinancial / Monetary

Treasury Doubles Bond Buybacks, Gold Jumps to 2.5-Month High

↑ Safe Haven Demand

Gold surged more than 4% Wednesday to an intraday high near $4,557/oz, its best level since early June, after the Treasury Department announced it would at least double buyback operations for 10-to-30-year securities to contain long-end borrowing costs. The move weakened the dollar and drove yields down before a partial reversal, but gold held most of its gains near $4,530 through Friday. Continued central bank gold purchases, particularly from China, and concerns that yield-curve intervention signals fiscal dominance are reinforcing the 'debasement trade' narrative.

↓ Cost Relief

Samsung, SK Hynix Sell Out 2027 DRAM/HBM Capacity

↓ Supply Deficit

Samsung's memory chief warned that 'significant shortages' across memory products will persist through at least 2027, echoing SK Hynix's own warning a week earlier, as both companies report demand fulfillment rates at record lows. DRAM contract prices have risen as much as 70% in recent quarters with spot prices for some parts up over 600%, while IDC projects DRAM and NAND supply growth of just 16-17% this year, far below the pace of AI-driven demand. Hyperscalers are redirecting wafer allocation from phones and PCs toward high-margin HBM for AI servers, squeezing consumer device makers.

Kansas Hits 112°F Record as Southern Plains Drought Devastates Crops

USDA's August 18 Agricultural Weather report shows Russell, Kansas hit a monthly-record 112°F on August 9 amid a month-long Southern Plains heat and drought event, with 44.3% of the US and 52.7% of the Lower 48 in drought as of August 18. Rangeland, pastures, and rain-fed summer crops continue to deteriorate under the heat even as Corn Belt flooding creates the opposite problem, with Ohio topsoil rated 52% surplus. Pro Farmer's Crop Tour flagged a 'deeply divided Corn Belt,' with trade estimates trimming corn yield toward 182.4 bushels per acre.

How Catalyst Works

STEP 03 🎯

A conviction score (1–10) is assigned on directness of the causal link, revenue exposure, and how much of the effect is already priced into the stock.

Deliver
